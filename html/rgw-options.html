Current Theme (Pushed): Tap/Untap Shenanigans (Saddle, Survival & Tap Triggers)

GW is genuinely strong with rares; Red & RG remain structurally thin

Adding rare signposts (Wylie Duke, Sunstar Chaplain, Ornery Tumblewagg, Kona, Hawkeye, Warden of the Inner Sky, Kirol) gives drafters real early-pick direction. However, GW still carries 75% of the payoff weight—Red's tap triggers are mostly rummagers or Mounts that act more like enablers, and RG's only options are Radha (which needs Domain) or Scarlet Spider (Web-slinging).

Alternative A: +1/+1 Counters & Modified (Backup, Outlast, Support & Scaling)

Top Recommendation — Deepest card pool, flashy rares, zero conflict with other 9 themes

None of the other 9 themes claim +1/+1 counters. Every color and guild pair in RGW has standout rare and uncommon signposts, and the enablers cross-pollinate naturally with WBG Lifegain (Ajani's Pridemate, Trelasarra), BRG Ferocious (growing creatures to Power 4+ by turn 3!), WUB Artifacts (Arcbound Condor), and GWU Blink (Backup / Support ETBs).

Alternative B: Attacking & Combat Triggers (Battalion, Pack Tactics & Raid)

Strong & Proactive — Bridges RWB Go-Wide and BRG Power 4+

Rewards turning creatures sideways every turn via 'Whenever you attack', Battalion (3+ attackers), and Pack Tactics / Formidable (total power 6+ or 8+). Acts as natural glue between RWB Go-Wide (which supplies extra bodies for Battalion) and BRG Behemoths (which supplies high power for Pack Tactics).

Alternative C: Heroic / Valiant & Combat Tricks (Target Matters)

High-Synergy Tempo — Bridges cleanly with URW Spellslinger

Combines classic Heroic/Repartee ('Whenever you cast a spell that targets...') with Bloomburrow's Valiant ('Whenever this becomes the target of a spell OR ability you control'). Because Valiant triggers on abilities too, Backup creatures, Saddle/Mount buffs, and tap abilities all trigger your payoffs alongside combat tricks.
