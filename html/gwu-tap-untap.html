GWU Tap/Untap — Variant 1: With 'Fey' Batch (Elves + Merfolk + Kithkin)

Huge Upgrade over RGW — Blue & WU/GU fix every hole Red had

Batching Elves, Merfolk, and Kithkin as 'Fey' bridges Ixalan/Lorwyn Merfolk (U, WU, GU) with Green/GW Elves. Dionus, Kumena, Merrow Reejerey, Deepchannel Duelist, Deepway Navigator, and Meanders Guide all scale off the entire batch, while Captain America and Wylie Duke reward every tap trigger regardless of creature type.

GWU Tap/Untap — Variant 2: Pure Mechanics (No Kindred Batch Needed)

Clean & Zero Stickers — Uses self-contained Merfolk + Survival/Saddle

Even without type batching, many of the best Merfolk tap cards (Adept Watershaper, Wanderbrine Trapper, Fallowsage, Surgespanner, Pestered Wellguard, Forensic Researcher, Kiora's Follower) don't care about creature types at all. Pairing Blue's tap/untap engines with GW's Saddle and Survival cards gives a super cohesive mechanical identity.
